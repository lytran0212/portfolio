# Design Guidelines for Thao Ly's Personal Portfolio

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from Japanese-influenced artistic portfolios and modern creative showcases, emphasizing the fusion of traditional aesthetics with contemporary web design.

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Navy Blue: 220 60% 20% (primary brand color)
- Red-Orange: 14 85% 60% (accent for highlights and CTAs)
- Cream: 60 25% 95% (background and text areas)

**Supporting Colors:**
- Deep Navy: 220 70% 15% (text and borders)
- Soft Cream: 45 20% 98% (card backgrounds)
- Muted Orange: 20 40% 70% (subtle accents)

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Google Fonts "Inter" for clean, modern readability
- **Accent Font**: Google Fonts "Playfair Display" for headings and artistic elements
- **Japanese Elements**: Google Fonts "Noto Sans JP" for any Japanese text

### Layout System
**Spacing**: Tailwind units of 4, 8, 16, and 24 (p-4, m-8, gap-16, etc.)
- Generous whitespace reflecting Japanese minimalism
- Asymmetrical layouts with intentional negative space
- Grid-based sections with artistic offset elements

### Component Library

**Navigation**: Minimal top navigation with Japanese-inspired typography
**Hero Section**: Full-viewport with artistic background treatment, centered content
**Cards**: Elevated cards with subtle shadows and rounded corners
**Progress Bars**: Custom-styled for software proficiency with artistic flair
**Timeline**: Vertical timeline with artistic milestones
**Mindmap**: Interactive circular layout for Profile Hub section

### Visual Treatment
**Background**: Subtle gradient overlay from cream to soft navy
**Gradients**: Gentle transitions between navy and cream tones
**Artistic Elements**: Incorporate geometric shapes reminiscent of Japanese design
**Typography Hierarchy**: Bold headings in Playfair Display, body text in Inter

## Content Strategy
**Sections (5 total)**:
1. **Hero**: Tagline "Art × Language × Technology" with artistic background
2. **Journey Timeline**: Visual timeline from 2022-2025 with key milestones
3. **Skills & Proficiency**: Interactive progress bars for software expertise
4. **Profile Hub**: Mindmap-style layout showcasing hobbies, goals, and skills
5. **Contact & Projects**: Showcase and connection points

## Images
**Hero Background**: Large artistic composition featuring Japanese-inspired elements in navy and cream tones, positioned behind text content
**Timeline Icons**: Small artistic icons representing each year's achievements
**Skill Icons**: Software logos integrated with progress visualization
**Profile Hub**: Central artistic element with branching connections

The design balances artistic expression with professional presentation, reflecting Thao Ly's unique position at the intersection of art, language, and technology.
